# Thank You Card Feature

## Overview

The Thank You Card feature is an electronic recognition system designed to celebrate colleagues who exemplify our company's Values & Credos through their work. This feature enables instant, public recognition that is visible to everyone across the organization, fostering a culture of appreciation and excellence.

## Purpose

This feature allows team members to instantly send an electronic thank-you card to any colleague who demonstrates outstanding alignment with our company Values & Credos. These cards are visible to everyone, ensuring that acts of excellence are seen, recognized, and celebrated across the organization.

## Benefits

- **Employee Recognition**: Provides a platform for acknowledging and appreciating outstanding contributions
- **Culture Reinforcement**: Reinforces company Values & Credos through visible recognition
- **Team Cohesion**: Strengthens team bonds by celebrating achievements together
